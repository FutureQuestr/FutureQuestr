# FutureQuestr

FutureQuestr is a free study planner for Android. It turns your week into a list of small tasks called Quests, and it rewards you for actually doing them.

## The main core feature

The main core feature is the AI planner.

You tell it about your week in plain words, something like "Maths test on Friday, free after 4pm, want to exercise three times", and it builds a full timetable of Quests for you. You look it over, change whatever you like, and add it to your week. Everything else in the app, the XP, levels, streaks and reminders, is there to help you stick to that plan.

## What it is for

Most planners hand you a blank page, and a blank page is exactly what a lot of people struggle with. If you already find it hard to organise your time, an empty calendar does not help.

FutureQuestr starts from what you tell it and gives you something to follow. Then it makes finishing tasks feel like progress. You earn XP, level up, build a streak and collect badges, but you never have to learn a game to use it. It is a study planner first.

## Who it is for

Anyone aged 16 to 19 who wants to get on top of their time. That means sixth form and college students, people revising for exams, and people fitting lessons around a part time job, training or a busy home life.

You do not need to be a gamer. The game parts are light. There are no characters to look after, no story and no competing with other people. If you do not like the sounds, there is a mute button at the top of the home screen.

FutureQuestr is for people aged 16 and over. It asks for your year of birth the first time you open it, and it keeps only whether you passed that check, not your birth year.

## What you can do

* Describe your week and let the AI planner build the timetable
* Add, edit, undo and delete your own Quests
* Earn XP for finishing a Quest on its day, level up, keep a daily streak and collect badges
* Do a few small daily challenges about studying, health and talking to people
* Turn on Focus Mode to cover other apps with a lock screen and quieten your notifications for a set time
* Get friendly reminders that do not nag you
* Search by name, day or time, and filter by type of Quest
* Share your upcoming week as text
* Use it offline, and sign in to keep your timetable in sync across your devices
* Export and import a backup file
* Choose 12 hour or 24 hour time, in light or dark mode

## How to use it

### Getting the app

1. Open the Releases page of this repository and download the APK file from the latest release.
2. Open the file on your phone. Android will ask whether to allow installs from your browser or files app. Allow it for this install.
3. Open FutureQuestr. You need Android 8 or newer.

### First time setup

1. Enter your year of birth.
2. Tap Sign in with GitHub. A short code appears and your browser opens. Type the code in, approve, then switch back to the app. You need a free GitHub account. It acts as your FutureQuestr account and keeps your timetable safe.
3. Work through the setup list. It asks for notifications, permission to run in the background and the Focus Mode switches. You can skip any of them and come back later. Choose your phone brand at the top so the instructions match your menus.

### Planning your week

1. Open the Plan tab.
2. Write about your week. Mention your subjects, deadlines, goals and when you are free.
3. Tap Plan my week.
4. Check the result, then tap Add all, or discard it and try again.

If the AI is busy, or you have used up the day's allowance, you get a ready made starter timetable instead, so you are never left with nothing.

### Doing your Quests

1. Your Quests live on the Quests tab. Tap the plus button to add your own, or tap a Quest to edit or delete it.
2. When a Quest starts, tap Done once you have finished it. You can only complete a Quest from its start time onward.
3. XP counts when you finish a Quest on its own day. How much you get depends on the length of the Quest and how demanding it is. Finishing on time, keeping a streak and the odd lucky bonus add a little more. There is a daily cap, so rushing through fake Quests does not pay off.
4. Open the Progress tab to see your level, your daily challenges, your week at a glance and your badges.

### Focus Mode

1. Open the Focus tab and pick how long you want to focus.
2. Tap Start. If someone opens another app, they see an App locked by FutureQuestr screen with an Exit button. Phone calls and messages still get through.
3. Finish the whole session for bonus XP. You can end early, but you will not get the reward.

### Your account and your data

The Account tab is where you can switch between 12 hour and 24 hour time, export or import a backup, check for updates, sign out, delete your data or delete your account. Updates are checked in the background. When a new version is ready, the app asks if you want to install it.

## Your data

Your Quests, XP, level, streak and badges are stored on your phone. While you are signed in, a copy is kept on our server so you can use your timetable on another device.

When you use the AI planner, the text you wrote is sent to the AI services that build your timetable. Please do not put personal details like full names or addresses into Quest titles or the planner.

Your GitHub details are used only to sign you in. You can delete your data, or your whole account, from the Account tab whenever you like.
