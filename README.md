
## Release notes

### v1.0.0

The first release.

* AI planner that builds a week of Quests from a plain description
* Quests with XP, levels, daily streaks, badges and daily challenges
* Focus Mode and friendly reminders
* Works offline, and syncs through your GitHub sign in
* Backup export and import
* Light and dark themes, and a 12 or 24 hour time setting

Known limits: Android only, a GitHub account is needed to sign in, and the free AI services have daily limits.

## What is coming next

Version 1.1 is planned to bring in your school or college timetable. You will be able to paste your lessons in as text, or read them from a photo of a printout, and the planner will build your Quests around them.

## Bugs and ideas

If something breaks, or you have an idea, open an issue on this repository. It helps a lot if you say which phone you have and which version of Android it runs.
